\curia{} resolves ncRNA orthology in embedding space rather than by raw sequence
identity, constrained to syntenic loci. Moving from \rnafm{} to \rinalmo{}
removes the per-window re-embedding that \rnafm{}'s context sensitivity forced,
making island alignment both faster and more accurate on true matches.
